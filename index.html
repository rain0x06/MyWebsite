About

I build, break,

Hi, I’m rain0x. I work around native tooling, file formats, game and platform internals, and automation.

I like taking opaque systems and turning them into software people can actually use—whether that means live flight telemetry, disk recovery, asset tooling, or a one-off parser for a format that was never documented.

Most of my work starts with a practical question and ends somewhere between a hex editor, a debugger, and a usable interface.
