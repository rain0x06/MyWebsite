About

I’m curious, and I like making things.

Most of my projects start with a question, a broken tool, or a small annoyance I can’t stop thinking about.

Hi, I’m rain0x. I build websites, desktop apps, and small tools for problems I find interesting.

I like figuring out how things work, especially when the answer isn’t obvious or the tools that already exist aren’t very helpful.

The fun part is the investigation. The important part is turning what I learn into something clear, useful, and easy for someone else to pick up.
